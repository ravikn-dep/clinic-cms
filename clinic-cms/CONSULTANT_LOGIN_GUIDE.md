## Setting or Changing Your Password

If you need to set a new password or change your existing one, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Log In to the System
1. Log in using either Manus OAuth or your current password
2. You will see the dashboard

### Step 2: Access Password Management
1. Look for your **user profile icon** in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar
2. Click on your name or the profile icon
3. A dropdown menu will appear

### Step 3: Click "Change Password"
1. From the dropdown menu, click **"Change Password"**
2. You will be taken to the Password Management page

### Step 4: Set or Change Your Password

**To Set a New Password (First Time):**
1. In the "New Password" field, enter your desired password (minimum 6 characters)
2. In the "Confirm Password" field, enter the same password
3. Click **"Set Password"**
4. You will see a success message

**To Change Your Existing Password:**
1. In the "Current Password" field, enter your existing password
2. In the "New Password" field, enter your new password
3. In the "Confirm New Password" field, enter the new password again
4. Click **"Change Password"**
5. You will see a success message

### Step 5: Log Out and Log Back In
1. After setting/changing your password, click your profile icon again
2. Click **"Sign out"**
3. Log back in using your new password at `/password-login`
